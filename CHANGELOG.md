## [0.1.0] - 2026-10-15
### Added
- CHSH estimators, shot-noise error propagation and visibility mixing.
- Qiskit circuits for Phi+ with an exact statevector CHSH calculation.
- Illustrative CHSH-based min-entropy bound.
- Simulation runner, `bell-lab` CLI and theory plots.
- Test suite, CI workflow and contributor documentation.
